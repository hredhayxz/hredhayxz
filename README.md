### 🚀 About Me

I am a passionate **Software Engineer** and **Flutter / Mobile Application Developer** with a solid foundation in Computer Science and algorithmic problem solving.

Currently, I work as a **Software Engineer at ZaviSoft** in partnership with **SteadFast Courier Ltd.** (Bangladesh's leading courier and logistics network). I specialize in building responsive, high-performance cross-platform mobile apps for Android and iOS using **Flutter** and **Dart**, working with modern state management, offline-first caching, and seamless RESTful API integrations.

- 💼 **Current Position**: Software Engineer @ [ZaviSoft](https://zavisoft.com) × [SteadFast Courier Ltd.](https://steadfast.com.bd)
- 📱 **Mobile Development**: High proficiency in **Flutter**, **Dart**, Riverpod 3, BLoC/Cubit, GetX, Clean Architecture principles, Isolates, Background Services, and native Android/iOS platform integrations.
- 📦 **Published Creator**:
  - 📱 **Google Play & Apple App Store**: Experience building and deploying production cross-platform mobile apps for Android & iOS (including [QwikQR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amhredhay.qwikqr) on Google Play).
  - 🎯 **pub.dev**: Creator of [`smart_isolate_manager`](https://pub.dev/packages/smart_isolate_manager) (concurrency & background isolate manager for Flutter/Dart).
- 🧠 **Strong DSA & Problem Solving**: Solid problem solving and data structure foundation in **C++** with active contest profiles across **Codeforces**, **CodeChef**, **HackerRank**, and **Toph**.
- 🌐 **Web Services & APIs**: Strong knowledge in RESTful APIs, JSON parsing, WebSocket client integrations, and clean application design.
- 📍 **Location**: Dhanmondi, Dhaka, Bangladesh • **Open to Remote and Relocation Opportunities**.
